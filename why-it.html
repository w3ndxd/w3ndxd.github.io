Why IT?

I have been interested in tech for as long as I can remember. Growing up, I was fascinated by PC hardware: what is inside a computer and how all the parts work together. I was also an active member of IT clubs, so tech was never only a hobby I did alone.

What fascinates me about computer science is that it connects everything: the hardware inside a PC, the operating system on top of it, and the programs, networks and websites that people use every day. In ICT I can learn how all these layers work together, from a processor running instructions to software and data that make a service work. I want to understand a system from the bottom up and then build on top of it myself.

Now I want to turn my interest into a profession. Almost everything around us runs on software and data, and I want to be one of the people who builds it. That is why I am studying ICT at HZ.

WHY: I want to understand and solve

I want the moment when a hard problem becomes clear to be part of my daily work. In ICT that kind of thinking has a big effect, because software and data are used in banks, hospitals, shops and factories.

HOW: with a plan and by doing

I make a plan, work on it every day and learn by building real things. I use the same method in my study: I look at how much work is left and divide it over the days I have. I also ask teachers and classmates for help early. How I keep this up is in my goals.

WHAT: ICT at HZ

I study ICT at HZ, where I build my skills step by step. This website is my first project: I built it myself with HTML and CSS, tested it on localhost and published it on GitHub. Where this leads is on my direction page.

Why HZ

HZ is a smaller university of applied sciences on the coast of Zeeland, with a personal approach: teachers and students know each other, and HZ works with real companies. The programme works on real cases for regional clients, with themes from Zeeland such as water, the energy transition and a liveable region. On the HZ website I read that students even work on the Hzensor, a small boat that sails through Zeeland without a crew. As someone who is still fairly new in the Netherlands, this fits me. HZ is also close to where I live, so I can focus on my study.

Why the HBO-ICT programme

The first two years are broad, so I can try software engineering, data science and IT consulting before I choose a track. As a beginner in programming I also like that the programme starts with the basics and lets me work on projects for real clients from the first year.

What I know about the field

ICT professionals use technology to solve problems and to improve how organisations work. The HZ programme describes three main areas, software engineering, data science and IT consulting, and says the main focus is on analytical and problem-solving skills. Typical jobs are software engineer, data scientist, business IT consultant and team lead. I compare the two areas I am most interested in on my direction page.

What I expect and want to learn

  • I expect a steep start, because I am a complete beginner in programming, and a lot of teamwork on real projects.
  • I want to learn object-oriented programming, how to build a web application with a framework, how to work with databases and Git, and how a team delivers software.
  • I want to achieve all 60 EC in year 1, a track choice based on knowledge and not on guessing, and a graduation project where I build something that works.

Long term: my route

  1. Year 1: pass all 60 EC and learn the basics of programming and project work (see my goals).
  2. Year 2: follow the common second year and choose my track: Software Engineering or Data Science.
  3. Year 3: do a minor of 30 EC and an internship in my track.
  4. Year 4: finish with a complex project and a graduation internship at a company.
  5. After that: start as a software engineer. I also keep a university route open: after this year I will check the admission requirements of a technical university such as TU Delft and talk to my student career coach about it.
