Why IT?

I chose ICT because it is the place where the thing I am best at, math and logical thinking, turns into things that people actually use. Almost everything around us runs on software and data, and I want to be one of the people who builds it. Below I explain my motivation in three layers: why I do this, how I work and what I do.

WHAT
HOW
WHY

WHY: I want to understand and solve

I am driven by understanding how things work. In math, the best moment is when a hard problem suddenly becomes clear because you found the right method. I want that feeling in my daily work. ICT is the field where this kind of thinking has the biggest effect, because software and data are used in banks, hospitals, shops and factories.

HOW: I learn by doing

Last year proved to me that this works. In about five months I learned Dutch up to B2 level and I passed the state exams NT2 programma II and vwo mathematics B. I also did the IELTS. I did not do this with talent alone: I made a plan and worked on it every day. I use the same method in this study. I look at how much work I still have left and plan how much I must do each day to finish in time. I want to be certain that I reach my goals, so I check my work more than once. Sometimes that costs me time, so I am learning when something is good enough (see my goals).

WHAT: ICT at HZ

I study ICT at HZ University of Applied Sciences and I build my skills step by step. This website is my first project: I built it myself with HTML and CSS, tested it on local host and published it on my GitHub profile.

What I know about the field

ICT is much more than writing code. Almost every organisation needs software and data, so ICT professionals work everywhere. At HZ the first two years are broad. At the end of my second year I choose a study track: Software Engineering or Applied Data Science & AI. I explain both in my direction page.

What I expect, what I want to learn

  • I expect to work on real projects in a team, to get feedback from teachers and to learn from code reviews of other students.
  • I want to learn to program well enough to build a real application myself, to work with Git, and to work with data and statistics, because that is where my love for math can be used.
  • I want to achieve all 60 EC this year and a well-founded choice for my track.

Why the HBO-ICT programme

  • It is hands-on: in my first year I already work on practical projects, and I also learn the theory behind them.
  • The first years are broad (programming, computer science, data, IT infrastructure), so I build a solid foundation before I choose a track at the end of year 2.
  • In the third year I do a minor and an internship in my chosen track, so I can practise on real problems before I start my career.

Why HZ

HZ is a smaller university of applied sciences on the coast of Zeeland. It is known for its personal approach: teachers and students know each other, and HZ works together with real companies and clients. On the HZ website I read that students even work on the Hzensor, a small boat that sails through Zeeland without a crew. That shows how practical the education is. As someone who is still fairly new in the Netherlands, this fits me. I make progress by asking teachers and teacher assistants for help and by learning from other students.

There is also a practical reason: HZ is close to where my parents live and where I have lived for the past year. I already know Zeeland and the cities around here, so I can focus on my study.

Where I want to be in the long term

  1. This year: I want to get all 60 EC for my propedeuse and find out which direction I enjoy the most.
  2. End of this year: I have to decide whether I continue at HZ or move to a university. Since high school I have wanted to study in the Netherlands, especially at TU Delft, because I am interested in a study that combines programming with math and maybe physics and hardware. Before I decide, I will check the admission requirements and talk to my student career coach.
  3. Year 3 (if I stay at HZ): a minor and an internship in my track, Software Engineering or Applied Data Science & AI.
  4. After my studies: a successful career in a technical field where I program and use math. I do not know the exact area yet. Simulation, data and AI, and software that works with hardware are things I want to look into.

You can read how I will get there in my goals.
