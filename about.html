About me

Hi, I'm Ahmet. I am from Turkey and I moved to the Netherlands about a year ago. Now I am a first-year ICT student at HZ. Math has always been my strongest subject, and I like problems that I can solve step by step. I learn fast and I find it easy to talk to new people and connect with them.

I like to do things properly, so I often check my work more than once. That is good for the quality, but it can make me slow. I work on this in my goals.

Moving to a new country taught me a lot about myself. I learned Dutch up to B2 level in six months. The table below shows what I achieved before HZ. For me it shows that when I set a goal, I make a plan and work on it every day. That is the attitude I want to bring to ICT.
