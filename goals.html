Goals and SWOT analysis

My goals

In the first year of HBO-ICT you need 60 EC for your propedeuse. The Study Advice boundary is 45 EC. 60 EC divided over 4 quartiles is about 15 EC per quartile.

When Goal How I measure it
This year Earn all 60 EC of the first year and never fall below the Study Advice boundary of 45 EC My study progress in Osiris: EC obtained after every grade
Every quartile Get about 15 EC per quartile by passing each exam at the first attempt, and keep my planning up to date The status of every exam in Osiris
End of this year Know my best next step: continue at HZ and choose a track, or move to a university A written decision with reasons, based on my grades and experience
Long term Work in a technical field where I program and use math Projects in my portfolio and an internship in the field

What I need to study successfully

I am going to improve my ICT skills by spending 2 to 3 hours a day refreshing my knowledge and working on my tasks. This is how I will do it:

  • Every week I write a list of priorities, so I always start with the most important things.
  • I set fixed times in my calendar for school tasks and separate times for daily things.
  • I track my hours in a checklist.
  • When an assignment is good enough, I hand it in instead of spending extra time on small details.
  • I ask teachers, teacher assistants, my student career coach and classmates for help early, and I use the feedback from code reviews to improve my code.
  • I check my study progress in Osiris after every grade, so I see in time if I need a resit to stay above 45 EC.

If I fail an exam, I make a plan for the resit within a week and check in Osiris what it means for my 45 EC. I also accept that my work can't be 100% perfect, because I am still a student and making mistakes is a normal part of learning ICT.

SWOT analysis

Strengths

  • Strong in math: 37 of 40 questions correct in the Turkish state exam, vwo math B passed
  • I learn fast: Dutch B2 within five months
  • Structured and analytical
  • I connect easily with new people
  • Good English (IELTS 7.5)

Weaknesses

  • I check my work more than twice, so I can spend too long on small details
  • I am still a beginner in programming

Opportunities

  • Help from teachers, teacher assistants and my student career coach
  • Learning from other students in code reviews
  • Projects and assignments to practise
  • The first years are broad, so I can try both tracks before I choose

Threats

  • Skipping my planning
  • Losing motivation when my code does not work
  • Video games and other hobbies taking too much time in a busy week
